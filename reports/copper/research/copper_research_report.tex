\section{Executive Summary}
This project built a reproducible pipeline to estimate whether the Iranian copper cathode
warehouse receipt trades at a premium or discount to a comparable domestic physical price.
It integrates the official IME certificate and physical-market feeds, LME cash copper, and
free-market USD/IRR. Raw source snapshots are preserved; canonical CSV files are refreshed
incrementally and atomically; derived outputs are rebuilt from raw data.

The principal result covers 169 certificate-trading days from 26 October 2025 to 26 July
2026. It uses 26 exact physical/certificate anchors and 143 interpolated days. The estimated
certificate-to-domestic-physical bubble averaged 5.17\pct, with a median of 6.13\pct, a
minimum of $-14.52\pct$, and a maximum of 23.70\pct. It was positive on 125 days and
negative on 44 days.

\section{Research Architecture and Data Governance}
The workspace separates collection, transformation, and presentation:
\begin{itemize}
\item immutable API and HTML evidence is retained under \code{data/raw};
\item canonical raw CSVs are refreshed only by documented collectors;
\item physical derivatives are written to \code{data/processed/physical}, bubble and model
outputs to \code{data/processed/bubble}, and other analytical tables to
\code{data/processed/analysis};
\item collectors are incremental, idempotent, validated, and atomic;
\item notebooks read source/processed data but never rewrite raw files;
\item shared IME logic resides in \code{shared/ime\_data}, while copper filters remain explicit.
\end{itemize}

\section{Data Sources and Collection Logic}
\subsection{Certificate}
The certificate collector uses the official IME CDC endpoint
\url{https://dataapi.ime.co.ir/api/CDC/CDCTrades}. It treats \code{CD1COP0001}
and \code{CopperCthd} as one continuous instrument (Commodity ID 14), refreshes a trailing
14-day window, merges by date, retains zero-volume days, and archives complete JSON responses.
The analytical price is \code{TodaySettlementPrice}. The independently calculated
\code{TradesValue/TradesVolume} is a validation only; the allowed difference is approximately
0.5 IRR because the official settlement is rounded.

The current raw certificate contains 246 calendar rows from 20 October 2025 through
2 August 2026, of which 178 have positive trading volume.

\subsection{Physical copper}
The official IME physical-market service is queried month by month. Full market responses are
archived before filtering. The canonical scope is intentionally narrow: goods exactly equal
to copper cathode, National Iranian Copper Industries Company, symbols \code{NCI-CCAA-00}
and \code{NCI-OACCAA-00}, and cash or cash-matching contracts. Forward, credit, other
producers, and ``copper cathode 2'' are excluded. Quantity-zero offers remain in raw data but
do not enter traded-price benchmarks.

The canonical physical CSV has 1,163 rows and 36 columns from 1387/06/03 through 1405/05/04;
1,154 rows are positive trades.

\subsection{LME and FX}
Westmetall annual tables are collected with field \code{LME\_Cu\_cash} through a workspace-wide
LME engine with explicit Copper configuration. It downloads history from 2008 on first run and
refreshes only the newest local year thereafter. Annual HTML
is preserved, and source ``-'' markers remain unchanged in raw. The current file contains 4,699
dates through 31 July 2026.

Complete IME physical responses are content-addressed once in the shared source archive; the
filtered Copper raw CSV remains project-owned. Generic as-of and regression mechanics are shared,
while cathode scope and benchmark assumptions remain explicit in Copper.

The shared canonical TGJU-derived FX series under \code{shared/data/raw/fx} stores IRR/USD.
New observations use the daily close; historical user-supplied midpoint observations are preserved and labeled
\code{legacy\_high\_low\_midpoint}. Calendar conversion is round-trip validated. The current
series contains 13,074 dates through 1405/05/31.

\section{Physical Benchmark}
Only rows with positive price and quantity enter the daily benchmark:
\begin{equation}
P_d=\frac{\sum_i p_{i,d}q_{i,d}}{\sum_i q_{i,d}}.
\end{equation}
Cash and cash-matching quantities, row counts, symbols, and matching share remain visible for
audit. On all 362 historical days with both mechanisms, their prices were equal. The builder
stops if they diverge in future data rather than silently continuing the one-price rule.
The output \code{nci\_copper\_cash\_daily.csv} contains 789 positive-trade days from
24 August 2008 through 26 July 2026.

\section{Intrinsic Price and Market Alignment}
LME and FX are joined as-of: the latest observation on or before each target date is selected,
never a future observation. Source date and age are retained. Intrinsic copper value is
\begin{equation}
I_t=\left(\frac{LME^{cash}_t}{1000}\right)FX_t,
\end{equation}
in IRR/kg. This transparent proxy excludes domestic tax, transport, storage, financing,
quality, delivery, and liquidity effects.

\section{Three Bubble Definitions}
\begin{align}
B^{P/I}_t&=100\left(\frac{P_t}{I_t}-1\right),\\
B^{C/I}_t&=100\left(\frac{C_t}{I_t}-1\right),\\
B^{C/P}_t&=100\left(\frac{C_t}{\widehat{P}_t}-1\right).
\end{align}
The first is the domestic physical basis to LME--FX. The second is the direct certificate
basis to LME--FX. The third is the principal certificate premium to estimated domestic
physical value. They answer different questions and must not be conflated.

\section{Primary Estimator: Ratio Interpolation}
On each exact physical/certificate anchor $a$, the domestic basis ratio is
$R_a=P_a/I_a$. Between consecutive anchors $a_L$ and $a_R$:
\begin{align}
w_t&=\frac{t-a_L}{a_R-a_L},\\
\widehat{R}_t&=R_{a_L}+w_t(R_{a_R}-R_{a_L}),\\
\widehat{P}_t&=\widehat{R}_t I_t.
\end{align}
Calendar-day interpolation is used. No extrapolation is permitted before the first or after
the last anchor. At anchors, estimated physical price must reconstruct observed price. This
method lets daily LME and FX movements enter intrinsic value while smoothing only the domestic basis.

\section{Experimental Regression}
Regression uses only intrinsic price as the feature and physical price as the target;
certificate price never enters model fitting. Five-fold \code{TimeSeriesSplit} compares a
proportional model, a linear model with intercept, and a degree-two ridge polynomial. The current
selection is
\begin{equation}
\widehat{P}=1{,}929{,}020.20+0.75842654 I.
\end{equation}
Only 26 anchor observations are available, so regression remains a sensitivity analysis and
ratio interpolation remains official.

\subsection{How to read the three charts together}
The charts form a valuation bridge. The first estimates the domestic physical basis to global
copper and FX. The second places the certificate against the same global factor. The third
removes the estimated domestic physical basis from that direct comparison. A negative second
chart can therefore coexist with a positive third chart: both the certificate and physical
copper may be below intrinsic value, while the certificate remains expensive relative to the
domestic physical commodity. Line segments should not be read as observations on every calendar
day: the physical chart joins sparse benchmark dates, and the principal chart contains modeled
interpolation between observed anchors.

\section{Validation and Audit Controls}
Controls cover required schema; stable identity and allowed codes; sorted unique dates;
Gregorian--Jalali round trips; non-negative raw values; positive traded benchmark inputs;
settlement/VWAP reconciliation; exact duplicate detection; immutable snapshots; atomic writes;
as-of source ages; exact anchor reconstruction; and no extrapolation. Five network-free copper
contract tests currently cover core raw and processing contracts.

\section{Outputs and Reproduction}
Principal files are \code{nci\_copper\_cash\_daily.csv},
\code{physical\_vs\_intrinsic\_bubble.csv},
\code{certificate\_vs\_intrinsic\_bubble.csv},
\code{copper\_certificate\_bubble.csv}, and \code{intrinsic\_regression.csv}.
Run collectors first, then the physical benchmark, intrinsic bubbles, principal bubble, and
regression. Processed files are rebuilt from current raw inputs and never modify raw data.

\section{Limitations and Next Steps}
The main limitation is the 26-anchor sample and its uneven timing, including a long physical
trade gap. Ratio interpolation assumes a smooth domestic basis between anchors. As-of joins may
carry stale weekend/holiday observations. Intrinsic value omits domestic frictions. A positive
bubble does not imply imminent convergence.

Next steps are monitored scheduling, freshness thresholds, broader regression tests, systematic
anchor-gap reporting, and repeated economic interpretation as more physical trades accumulate.
