\textbf{\color{navy}Completed and deployed --- routine updates only}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Commodity certificates:} Finalized bubble and premium calculations for \textbf{copper, zinc, pellet, rebar, bitumen, and pistachio}, with charts and Power BI outputs. Copper and zinc include comparisons with domestic physical prices and international values; the other products compare certificates with their physical-price references. Sources: Iran Mercantile Exchange, LME, TGJU, and Abtahi Pistachio. All six products support \textbf{one-click updates}.
  \item \textbf{Warehouse fees:} Prepared historical daily storage-fee series by commodity from official IME notices, fee tables, and archived official pages. Completed and available for one-click updates.
  \item \textbf{National Copper / Codal:} Collected company disclosures and calculated standalone quarterly revenue, gross profit, and net profit histories. The requested time series are delivered, with one-click updates.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\color{navy}Three projects remain in progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Housing:} Collected and processed housing, macroeconomic, and related securities data from CBI, SCI, and TSETMC, including housing/rent inflation and mortgage-certificate price series. Subsequently redesigned the project and began rebuilding the datasets in an organized structure. Four core datasets are now established; reconstruction of the remaining collections is ongoing.
  \item \textbf{Economic USD:} Prepared the USD/IRR, Iranian and U.S. CPI, liquidity, and combined monthly datasets. The remaining valuation stage uses dollar liquidity and PPP, with five PPP anchors calculated from six-to-twelve-month averages rather than single dates.
  \item \textbf{Asset allocation:} Calculated monthly returns, risk comparisons, portfolio allocations, and sensitivity results for housing, gold, equities, and fixed income using CBI, TGJU, and TSETMC data. Calculations, report, and presentation are complete. The \textbf{survey is under construction and must be completed by respondents} to finish the project.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\color{navy}Closed projects and reasons}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Global copper / COCHILCO:} Prepared an international copper database and research framework covering production, inventories, and market activity from COCHILCO, BGS, USGS, CFTC, CME, SHFE, IRENA, and Chinese official statistics. The broader project was \textbf{cancelled because its scope was too large}.
  \item \textbf{Iran Energy Exchange:} Collected certificate histories and assessed trading volume, frequency, and participation. The project was \textbf{closed because trading volume was low and our company's investment unit could not practically buy and sell in that market}.
  \item \textbf{Ahrom options:} Assembled the contract inventory and available daily price histories from TSETMC and OptionBaaz and delivered a collection tool. The original research study is closed.
\end{itemize}
